Reliable data transfer simulator: rdt1.0 → rdt3.0

Each run is a real simulation of the chosen version's sender and receiver finite-state machines (Kurose §3.4.1) over a channel whose faults you choose. Time flows downwards. Black arrows are data packets, blue arrows are ACK/NAK packets, red means corrupted, a dashed ochre arrow ending in ✗ means lost, and the ochre bar beside the sender is the countdown timer.

Use ok, corrupt, lose or delay (first letters work). Transmissions beyond your list are delivered normally. “delay” makes that packet take longer than the timeout (rdt3.0 only); rdt2.x has no timer, so a loss deadlocks it.
