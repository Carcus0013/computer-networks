TCP congestion control: slow start, congestion avoidance, fast recovery

Round-based model, as used in exam questions: in each transmission round (one RTT) the sender transmits cwnd segments. Loss events happen at the end of the rounds you list. Step through round by round, or press Play to watch the graph draw.

A loss listed at round r is detected at the end of that round, so the new cwnd applies from round r + 1. ssthresh is halved with rounding down.
