Pipelined reliable transfer: Go-Back-N vs Selective Repeat

Packets are sent back to back (one every 0.25 time units) and take 1 time unit to cross the channel; ACKs take 1 unit back. The window boxes on the right show the sender's and receiver's view of the sequence numbers at each step.

“2” loses the first transmission of packet 2; “5:2” loses its second transmission. ACK losses work the same way for the ACK carrying that number.
