Measuring RTT and setting TCP's timeout

Each segment's SampleRTT is the time from sending it to receiving its ACK. TCP smooths the samples into EstimatedRTT, tracks their variability as DevRTT, and sets TimeoutInterval = EstimatedRTT + 4·DevRTT. The ochre bar beside the sender is the timer that was armed when the segment left; if a sample is longer than it, a premature timeout would have occurred.

Try a sudden spike such as “100, 100, 100, 100, 180” to see a premature timeout, or the uploaded Chapter3-Problems values: initial 390 / 21, samples 250, 200, 360 with RFC order.
