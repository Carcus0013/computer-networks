TCP: handshake, sequence and ACK numbers, flow control, and close

A complete TCP connection between a client and a server: the three-way handshake with real initial sequence numbers, the client sending a file in MSS-sized segments under the receiver's advertised window (flow control), and the four-segment close with TIME_WAIT. The boxes on the right track the send window and the receive buffer.

Sequence numbers count bytes. SYN and FIN each use up one sequence number.
