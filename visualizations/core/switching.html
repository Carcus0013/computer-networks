Packet switching (store-and-forward) vs circuit switching

A space–time diagram: each shaded band is one packet being transmitted on a link (its height is the transmission time L/R) and the slope is the propagation delay. With store-and-forward, a router can start sending a packet only after it has received all of it. A circuit instead streams bits straight through at its reserved share of the link.

Defaults follow the message-segmentation problem with 4 packets so the bands are visible; set Packets to 800 to reproduce the exam answer (the diagram then summarises).
