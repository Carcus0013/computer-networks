The self-learning switch: learn, flood, filter, forward

Each frame teaches the switch where its source lives. Frames to unknown destinations are flooded; frames to known destinations go out of exactly one port; frames whose destination is on the incoming port are filtered.

The default sequence is 19CSE301 Jan 2023 Q10. Use “B>*” for a broadcast frame.
